Every OpenComputer organization has its own computer. It holds your
organization's repositories, runs coding sessions for your team, and keeps
everything on its disk between sessions. You talk to it from Slack.

Open **Computer** in the dashboard at
[app.opencomputer.dev](https://app.opencomputer.dev). The first time you open
it, OpenComputer sets up your computer; this takes a few minutes.

## Get started

The Computer page walks you through four steps.

1. **Computer ready.** The page shows your computer's status and its capacity:
   vCPUs, memory and disk.
2. **Install the Slack app.** Select **Install Slack app** and approve
   OpenComputer for your Slack workspace. One Slack workspace links to one
   OpenComputer organization. To stop using a workspace, select **Unlink**;
   the app stays installed in Slack until you remove it there.
3. **Connect GitHub.** Select **Connect GitHub** to install the OpenComputer
   GitHub App and choose which repositories it can access. Your computer works
   in exactly the repositories you grant. You can change the selection at any
   time in GitHub, or from **Connections** in the dashboard.
4. **Start a session.** Mention `@OpenComputer` in any channel, or send it a
   direct message. Each Slack thread is one session.

## Sessions

The Computer page lists your organization's sessions with their status and
when they last changed. **Open in Slack** takes you to the session's thread,
where you continue the conversation.

| Status   | Meaning                                   |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Starting | The session is starting on your computer. |
| Working  | The computer is working on a request.     |
| Idle     | The session is waiting for your next message. |
| Ended    | The session has finished.                 |
| Error    | The session stopped because of an error.  |

## Computer status

| Status     | Meaning                                                                 |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setting up | OpenComputer is preparing your computer.                                |
| Online     | Your computer is on and ready for work.                                 |
| Offline    | Your computer is on but not connected. It reconnects by itself.         |
| Sleeping   | Your computer is asleep. It wakes when you message it.                  |
| Waking     | Your computer is starting again, with everything on its disk intact.    |
| Failed     | Something went wrong. The page shows what happened and lets you retry.  |

On the free plan, a computer that has been idle for a while goes to sleep and
wakes up when someone messages it; waking takes a minute or two. Paid plans
keep the computer always on.

## Build your own agents

Your computer is where your team works interactively. To build agents that run
on their own, on a schedule, from webhooks or in Slack, follow the
[quickstart](/agents/quickstart).
